Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What we collect

Audio files and transcripts you upload are processed in memory and deleted immediately after alignment completes. We do not store, log, or retain your audio files, transcripts, or alignment results on our servers.

2. Third-party services

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

3. Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies for session management (Flask session cookie) and for AdSense ad delivery. No tracking cookies are set by this site beyond what AdSense requires.

4. Data retention

Temporary upload files are deleted within one hour. Server logs may retain IP addresses for operational purposes and are automatically rotated and deleted within 7 days.

For questions about this policy, contact us.
